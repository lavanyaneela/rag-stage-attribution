# Laptop Refresh Program

Standard issue laptops are refreshed every three years counted from the asset's first assignment date. The refresh is initiated by IT; employees do not need to request it. Keeping a laptop past the refresh date requires an exception ticket renewed every six months.

Engineers and data scientists receive the high memory configuration by default. All other roles receive the standard configuration unless their manager requests otherwise at refresh time. Configuration upgrades between refresh cycles are granted only for documented workload needs.

A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to Security within 24 hours of discovery. Reported devices are remotely wiped and their certificates revoked immediately. Replacement hardware ships within two business days for employees in supported regions.

Damaged laptops are repaired when the repair cost is below half the replacement cost, otherwise replaced. Accidental damage does not affect an employee's standing; repeated losses are reviewed by Security. Old devices are wiped and recycled through the certified disposal vendor.

Operating system choice is Linux or macOS for engineering roles and macOS or Windows elsewhere. Dual booting and removing disk encryption are both prohibited. Disk encryption status is verified daily by the device management agent.
