# Learning and Training Budget

Each employee has a $1,200 annual learning budget for courses, books, conferences, and certifications. The budget follows the calendar year and unused budget does not roll over. Purchases go through the learning platform where possible so licenses are tracked.

Conference attendance uses the learning budget for the ticket and the travel policy for the trip. One conference per year can exceed the budget with director approval when the employee is speaking. Speaking engagements approved this way include one travel day on work time.

Certifications relevant to the current role are reimbursed including one exam retake. The retake allowance exists so that an exam attempt is a learning event rather than a gamble. Certification maintenance fees in later years come from the same annual budget.

Team training purchased centrally does not draw from individual budgets. Managers propose team training through the quarterly planning cycle. Mandatory compliance training is always centrally funded and never touches the learning budget.

Study time of up to four hours per month during work hours is available with manager awareness. The time is for budgeted learning items, not general reading. Unused study time does not accumulate.
