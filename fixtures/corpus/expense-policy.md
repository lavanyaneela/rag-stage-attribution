# General Expense Policy

Meridian reimburses reasonable business expenses submitted through the Concur portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted after 30 days require written approval from the employee's cost center owner. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursable under any approval path.

Itemized receipts are required for any single expense above $25. Credit card statements are not accepted as receipts for purchases above that line. For expenses of $25 or below, the portal accepts a short written justification instead of a receipt.

Meals with clients are reimbursable up to $60 per person per meal. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client meals and is capped at two drinks per attendee. Meals among employees without a client present fall under the team events budget, not the expense policy.

Software purchases under $200 per year may be expensed directly with manager approval. Anything above $200 per year must go through vendor onboarding and procurement, regardless of how the vendor bills. Splitting an annual subscription into monthly charges to stay under the line is a policy violation.

Mileage for personal vehicle use on company business is reimbursed at $0.62 per mile. Parking and tolls are reimbursed at cost with receipts. Commuting between home and the assigned office is never reimbursable.

Expense reports are approved by the direct manager and audited quarterly by Finance. Three rejected claims in a rolling year triggers a mandatory policy refresher. Deliberate misstatement of an expense is a terminable offense.
