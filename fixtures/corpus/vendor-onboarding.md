# Vendor Onboarding

Vendor onboarding is the commercial and legal gate for any new supplier, owned by Procurement. Onboarding collects tax forms, banking details, insurance certificates, and the signed master agreement. No purchase order is issued before onboarding completes.

Onboarding for a vendor with standard terms takes five business days from complete paperwork. Negotiated terms route through Legal and commonly take three to six weeks. Backdating a start date to before onboarding completion is prohibited.

Any engagement above $200 per year requires onboarding regardless of billing cadence, mirroring the expense policy line. Below that line, a one time purchase may be expensed directly. The threshold is evaluated per vendor per year, not per invoice.

Renewals above a 7 percent year over year price increase require a competitive check before signature. The competitive check is waived when switching costs are documented as exceeding the increase. Auto renew clauses longer than 12 months are struck by default in negotiation.

Vendors are offboarded when a contract ends: access revoked within five business days, data return or destruction confirmed in writing, and the inventory entry closed. The security review record is retained after offboarding under the data retention schedule. Re engaging a previously offboarded vendor restarts onboarding from the beginning.
