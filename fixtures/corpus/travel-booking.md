# Travel Booking

All business travel is booked through the Navan platform so that itineraries, approvals, and duty of care tracking stay in one place. Booking outside the platform is allowed only when the platform shows no availability, and requires attaching a screenshot of the failed search. A trip booked outside the platform without that evidence is reimbursed at the platform's best comparable fare.

Trips require a travel request before booking: the request names the business purpose, the dates, and the estimated cost. Requests under $1,500 are approved by the direct manager; above that they also route to the department head. The request ID is what makes a trip an approved trip.

Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance where the trip is foreseeable. The platform flags bookings inside seven days for an extra approval step. Refundable fares are chosen only when the trip has a known cancellation risk recorded on the request.

Hotel bookings default to the negotiated partner properties shown first in the platform. Loyalty points earned on business stays belong to the traveler. Early checkout fees from schedule changes are a reimbursable travel cost.

Visa fees, entry fees, and required vaccinations for business travel are covered by the company. The travel desk handles visa paperwork for trips longer than 30 days. Passport renewal is a personal cost even when prompted by business travel.
