# On-Call Rotation

Every production service is covered by an on-call rotation of at least four engineers. A rotation shift lasts seven days and begins Monday at 10:00 local time for the primary region. Engineers swap shifts through the scheduler, not through direct messages, so coverage is always auditable.

The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 10 minutes. If a page is not acknowledged in 10 minutes it escalates automatically to the secondary. The secondary has a further 10 minutes before the page reaches the engineering manager.

On-call compensation is a flat $450 per rotation week plus hourly pay for time spent responding outside business hours. Responding time is logged against the incident ticket, not estimated afterward. Weeks with zero pages still earn the flat rate.

New team members shadow two full rotation weeks before taking a primary shift. Shadowing means receiving every page the primary receives and joining every response call. An engineer may not hold primary on two rotations in the same week.

Rotation health is reviewed monthly. A rotation averaging more than five pages per week for a quarter must file a remediation plan with its director. Pages caused by known false positives count toward that average on purpose, because alert hygiene is the rotation's responsibility.
