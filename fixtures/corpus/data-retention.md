# Data Retention Schedule

Customer account records are retained for seven years after account closure to satisfy contractual audit rights. Retention clocks start at closure, not at the last activity. Early deletion requires written sign-off from Legal.

Application logs containing user identifiers are retained for 90 days and then deleted by an automated job. Aggregated metrics derived from those logs may be kept indefinitely because they contain no identifiers. The deletion job publishes a weekly report of purged volumes.

Email and chat messages are retained for three years. Placing a legal hold suspends deletion for the named custodians until the hold is released. Legal holds override every other line in this schedule.

Candidate interview records are retained for two years after the hiring decision. Rejected candidate records past two years are deleted in the quarterly purge. Hired candidate records move into the employee file and follow employment retention rules instead.

Database backups are not an archive and follow the backup schedule, not this document. Restoring deleted data from backups to circumvent retention is prohibited. Requests to retrieve purged data are logged and require Legal approval even when technically possible.
