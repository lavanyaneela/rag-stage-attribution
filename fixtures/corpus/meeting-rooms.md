# Meeting Rooms and Desk Booking

Desks are booked through the Workplace app up to 14 days ahead, and a booking releases automatically if the badge does not tap in by 10:30. Teams can reserve neighborhoods for anchor days agreed with Workplace. Unbooked desks are first come, free to use.

Meeting rooms are booked from the calendar and release after 10 minutes of no show, detected by the room sensor. Recurring holds on rooms larger than eight seats are reviewed monthly and trimmed when attendance is routinely lower. Phone booths are never bookable and are for calls under an hour.

Rooms with video equipment are labeled by tier in the calendar. Tier one rooms carry dual screens and tracking cameras for hybrid meetings. Equipment faults are reported through the panel on the room door and triaged by Workplace within one business day.

Customer meetings take precedence over internal meetings in the customer suite rooms. The customer suite is booked through reception, not through the calendar. Catering requests for the suite need two business days of notice.

Whiteboard walls are cleaned nightly unless marked with a save sticker, and saved content is photographed to the team's shared drive on request. Save stickers expire after five working days. Confidential material is never left on a whiteboard overnight in shared rooms.
