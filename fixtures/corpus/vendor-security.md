# Vendor Security Review

Every vendor that stores or processes Meridian data completes a security review before any data flows. Reviews are valid for two years, after which the vendor is re reviewed. A lapsed review suspends new data flows but does not interrupt existing contracts mid term.

The review tier depends on data classification: vendors touching customer data undergo the full assessment, vendors touching only internal business data complete the standard questionnaire. The full assessment includes an architecture interview and evidence of an independent audit within the past 12 months.

Findings are rated critical, high, medium, or low. A critical finding blocks the engagement until remediated and re verified. High findings require a remediation plan with dates before contract signature; medium and low findings are tracked but do not block.

Security review and vendor onboarding are different gates: onboarding covers commercial and legal terms, this review covers only security posture. Passing one gate says nothing about the other. Procurement will not issue a purchase order until both gates are green.

Sub processors added by a vendor after signature must be disclosed within 30 days of their introduction. An undisclosed sub processor discovered in audit is a contract breach escalated to Legal. The vendor inventory lists every approved vendor with its review expiry date.
