# Corporate Card Program

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times a year or regularly host clients. Card issuance requires manager approval and a short training module. The card is for business spend only; personal charges must be flagged and repaid within one statement cycle.

Card transactions sync into the Concur portal nightly and still require receipts under the same lines as out of pocket expenses. A card does not change what is reimbursable, only how it is paid. Unsubmitted card transactions older than 60 days lock the card automatically.

Monthly card limits default to $5,000 and can be raised temporarily for an approved trip. Limit raises expire automatically when the trip window ends. Cash advances on the corporate card are disabled for all cardholders.

Lost or stolen cards are reported to the issuing bank first and then to Finance within one business day. The bank reissues within a week; Finance can grant a temporary virtual card in the meantime. Fraudulent charges disputed through the bank do not count against the employee.

Leaving the company or moving to a role without travel ends card eligibility. Cards are cancelled on the last working day and any pending transactions are submitted beforehand. Finance reconciles the final statement with the departing employee's manager.
