# Office Network and Guest Wi-Fi

Office networks are segmented into corporate, lab, and guest zones with no direct routing between them. Corporate zone access requires a managed device with a valid certificate, the same certificate the VPN uses. The lab zone is for test hardware and has no path to production.

Guest Wi-Fi is open to visitors with a rotating password displayed at reception. The guest password rotates every 14 days. Guest traffic reaches the public internet only and is rate limited per device.

Employees may not bridge zones with personal equipment. Plugging a personal router or switch into an office port disables that port automatically. Re enabling a disabled port requires a ticket to IT with a manager's acknowledgment.

Printers live in the corporate zone and are not reachable from guest Wi-Fi. Visitors who need to print send documents to their host, who prints from a managed device. This is a data handling rule, not a technical limitation.

Office network changes are made only during the Thursday maintenance window between 19:00 and 22:00 local time. Emergency changes outside the window require Security approval and a retroactive change record within one business day.
