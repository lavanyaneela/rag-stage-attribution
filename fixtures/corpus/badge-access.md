# Badge and Building Access

Employee badges open the office doors matching the employee's assigned site and any site they visit with an active desk booking. Badge taps are logged and the log is retained under the security data schedule. Lending a badge to anyone, including another employee, is prohibited.

A forgotten badge gets a one day paper pass from reception after identity verification. Three paper passes in a month triggers a reminder from Workplace. A lost badge is deactivated the moment it is reported and a replacement is printed the same day.

Visitors are registered by their host before arrival and are escorted in all non public areas. Visitor badges expire at 18:00 on the day of issue. Contractors on long engagements receive photo badges with an end date matching their contract.

Server room access is a separate entitlement on top of building access and requires the infrastructure director's approval. The entitlement is reviewed quarterly and expires if unused for 90 days. Tailgating into the server room is treated as a security incident, not an etiquette lapse.

After hours building access between 22:00 and 06:00 is recorded and reviewed weekly by Workplace. Repeated after hours access without a stated operational reason prompts a manager check in. The policy exists for safety, not for monitoring productivity.
