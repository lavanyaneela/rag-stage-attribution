# Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 18 weeks of fully paid leave. Non birthing parents and adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave may begin up to two weeks before the expected date of birth or placement.

Parental leave may be taken in one block or split into at most two blocks used within the first year. Both blocks must be scheduled with the manager at least four weeks before each begins. Intermittent single days are handled under PTO, not parental leave.

Benefits continue unchanged during parental leave, and PTO accrual continues at the normal rate. Performance reviews falling inside a leave are deferred, never skipped, and leave is excluded from any performance calculation. Promotion cycles proceed with the employee considered in absentia.

The first two weeks after return may be worked at half schedule at full pay as a ramp back. The ramp back is opt in and requires no approval beyond notifying the manager. On-call duties resume no earlier than four weeks after return.

Leave for pregnancy loss is two weeks, fully paid, available to either parent, and requires no documentation. This leave is separate from sick time and parental leave balances. HR handles these requests directly without manager involvement when asked.
