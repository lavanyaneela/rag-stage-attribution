# Travel Expense Exceptions

Travel expenses follow the general expense policy except where this document states otherwise. Where the two documents disagree for a cost incurred during an approved trip, this document wins. A trip is approved when it has a travel request ID issued before departure.

On approved travel, itemized receipts are required only for expenses above $75. Below that line the Concur portal accepts the card transaction record alone. This higher receipt line exists because small foreign receipts are frequently unreadable or unavailable.

The meal limit on travel days is $85 per day in total rather than a per meal cap. The daily limit covers all meals and incidental snacks. Client meals during travel still follow the $60 per person client meal rule and do not count against the traveler's daily limit.

Economy class is required for flights under six hours of scheduled duration. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires VP approval regardless of duration and is otherwise out of policy.

Lodging is capped at $240 per night in standard cities and $340 per night in the high cost city list maintained by Finance. Booking above the cap requires the travel desk to confirm no compliant option existed within two miles of the work site.

Ground transport on travel is reimbursed at cost, including rideshare. Rental cars require the compact or intermediate tier unless three or more employees share the vehicle. Mileage on a rental car is never claimed, only fuel.
