# Paid Time Off

Full time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, accruing monthly from the start date. Accrual is capped at 30 days; once the balance reaches the cap, accrual pauses until the balance drops. The cap exists to encourage actually taking the time.

PTO requests are submitted in the HR portal and approved by the direct manager. Requests of five or more consecutive working days should be submitted at least three weeks in advance. Managers may not deny PTO solely for coverage reasons more than twice in a row for the same employee.

Up to five unused days may be carried into the first quarter of the next year and expire on March 31. Carryover days are spent before newly accrued days. PTO is paid out at termination where local law requires it, and otherwise follows the local addendum.

Company holidays are published each November for the following year and do not draw from PTO. An employee required to work a company holiday receives a replacement day to be used within 90 days. Replacement days are tracked in the HR portal like regular PTO.

Sick time is separate from PTO and is not capped. Sick absences of more than three consecutive working days require a clinician's note in jurisdictions where requesting one is lawful. Sick time cannot be used to extend a planned vacation.
