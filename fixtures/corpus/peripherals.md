# Peripheral and Accessory Requests

Each employee has a $400 peripheral budget per refresh cycle for monitors, keyboards, docks, and similar accessories. The budget resets when the laptop is refreshed, not on a calendar schedule. Unspent budget does not carry over past a refresh.

Peripheral orders go through the IT catalog, which ships to office or home. Items outside the catalog require a ticket with a one line justification and are approved by IT, not by the employee's manager. Ergonomic equipment prescribed by a clinician bypasses the budget entirely.

Monitors above 32 inches are stocked for accessibility needs and shared desks only. A second external monitor is in the standard catalog for every role. Monitor arms, risers, and cables are treated as consumables and do not count against the peripheral budget.

Headsets certified for the conferencing platform are in the catalog in wired and wireless versions. Wireless headsets are replaced at most every two years. Batteries in wireless accessories are recycled through the office collection points, never through general waste.

Home office furniture is not a peripheral and follows the remote work stipend policy owned by HR. The peripheral budget cannot be converted to cash or transferred between employees. Leavers return catalog items above $100 in original value; smaller items are not collected.
